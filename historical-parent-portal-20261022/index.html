03 / SE’KRETS 2 TELL

A bridge, never a back door.

Parent Bridge is designed for moments a teen intentionally chooses to share with a verified guardian. It is not a way to read private pages, voice notes, messages, or moods.

Teen decidesSharing must be explicit, specific, and revocable.
Guardian verifiesIdentity, relationship, and permission must be checked by the server.
Private stays privateNo private journaling or safety details are exposed by default.
🔒 Account linking and child-specific information are currently unavailable. No private child information is accessible on this site.
